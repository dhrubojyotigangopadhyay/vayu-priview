FAQ
Questions answered.
Do I need to enter any API keys?
No. VAYU OS manages all AI infrastructure centrally. You connect Gmail (1 click), scan a WhatsApp QR code, and upload your CSV. That's it.
How does the WhatsApp connection work?
Click "Connect WhatsApp", a QR code appears. Scan it with your phone's WhatsApp. The session is stored securely server-side — no Business API needed.
What happens after my 7-day trial ends?
All AI agents pause automatically. Your data stays intact. Upgrade to continue — no data loss, ever.
How is VAYU OS different from Apollo or Instantly?
Those are tools. VAYU OS is an operating system. It handles email, WhatsApp, voice, inbox, and AI insights — without you touching any settings.
